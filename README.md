## Beyond the background

Your wallpaper should feel like more than a flat image.

**8PAPER** transforms ordinary pictures into dynamic depth wallpapers.  
Foreground and background react differently to your device’s movement, creating the illusion of real space directly on your home screen.

No complicated editing software.  
No endless configuration.

Just choose an image, adjust the depth and make it yours.
